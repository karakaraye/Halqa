# Halqa — product overview

This overview states what the product does; nothing here claims results that have not been measured.

## What it is

Halqa connects an actionable screening result to a responsible team, a visible next step, and a documented closure across screening, primary care, specialist services and the patient.

## The problem

Issuing a result does not show that the patient understood it, that a specialist accepted the referral, that the appointment happened, or that care followed. Split records and unclear ownership hide these gaps.

## What it does

- One case per actionable result, followed from issue to closure.
- One named owner and one clock for every step; hand-overs only count once accepted.
- Patients get their result in their own language, in plain words, with a short understanding check; if they are unsure, a person calls.
- Specialists must accept a referral or send it back with a reason; it cannot disappear.
- A licensed clinician signs every closure with a report reference and a plan. Closing without a test result needs a second, different licensed person.
- The system raises alerts for late steps; it never completes a medical step or closes a case itself.
- Measured figures show time to tell patients, tests on time, cases fully finished, understanding, and where patients get stuck.

## Who uses it

Care navigators (keep cases moving), doctors (decide and close), specialists (accept or send back), the clinic lead (approvals and oversight), and patients (their own result, steps and help).

## Boundaries

Time targets and urgency rules need clinical approval. Translations need certified review. The system does not replace the patient record or the lab system; it receives results and tracks what happens next. No improvement in outcomes is claimed until it is measured against real baseline data.

## Pilot design

With a screening programme, a coordinating clinical team and a receiving service, measure unresolved follow-up and delay before and after introduction, with endpoints agreed in advance, workload measured, and patient understanding measured. Do not claim savings or health benefit before measuring.
